# Define meaningful progression without levels

Type: grilling

## Question

What concrete actions constitute short-, medium-, and long-term progress without XP levels, numerical gates, or obsolete starter creatures? Specify meaningful advancement for combat, collection, exploration, and crafting, distinguish options from raw power, and test a newcomer/veteran scenario.
