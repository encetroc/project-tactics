# Define renewable economic demand without defeat losses

Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Mode: HITL
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

What recurring needs make creature farming, loot, crafting, and trading worthwhile when everyone can do everything, players lose nothing on defeat, and creatures do not level? Use example producer/buyer transactions and identify sources, sinks, and saturation risks. Preserve optional trade and organically earned efficiency rather than profession locks; do not assume mandatory creature destruction.
