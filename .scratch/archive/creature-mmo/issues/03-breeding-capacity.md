# Define predictable breeding and finite breeding capacity

Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Mode: HITL
Status: open
Blocked by: 02

## Question

Given the genetic model, what are the visible inheritance probabilities, player controls, randomness, costs, and limits on each creature's breeding capacity? Determine whether or how capacity can recover or transfer, and test whether the rules permit useful targeted breeding without endless perfect-stat rerolling or an unavoidable generational power ladder.
