# Define genome expression into a combat kit

Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Mode: HITL
Status: open
Blocked by: 02

## Question

How does the agreed abstract genetic representation deterministically generate a creature's born abilities, passives, and stats? Work from the candidate structure agreed in [Define a deep but readable genetic model](02-readable-genetics.md): one combined ordered, variable-length circular genome, context-dependent meanings, gene-specific influence curves with shapes still open, and integrated stat/ability/passive generation. Define interactions that create meaningful synergy and whether/how inherited material remains unexpressed. Specify distance/direction, repeated encounters including self-interaction, and influence aggregation; do not assume a required curve shape or separate genome-wide exception. The user has explicitly removed gene capacity; do not reintroduce it under another name. Establish how players inspect and predict results without requiring manual algorithm execution. Use contrasting worked genomes to test synergy, awkward combinations, and the intended short-genome specialization versus long-genome versatility tendencies; test whether either length or repeated self-interaction produces universal dominance. Extreme results are allowed: seek natural strategic counterplay, not mandatory saturation, hard power caps, or forced intrinsic weaknesses. Include concrete counter-strategies and usable explanations of contextual dependencies. These tendencies and natural counterbalance are hypotheses, not established consequences; revisit the candidate structure if they fail. Use the creature model and expandable core stats adopted in [Define creatures before their genes](11-creatures-before-genes.md), including separate AP/MP budgets and no adopted third resource; coordinate the expression interface with [Complete combat rules around genome-driven abilities](09-complete-combat-rules.md). Breeding probabilities and evolution mechanisms are separate decisions.
