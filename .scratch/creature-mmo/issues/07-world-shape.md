# Decide the gameplay purpose of a wraparound isometric world

Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Mode: HITL
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

Is wraparound geography a core gameplay requirement or a presentation preference, what does it contribute to exploration, biomes, travel, and trade, and is 3D-isometric presentation a constraint or a hypothesis? Decide the intended player-facing experience and what the first experiment needs to represent, not an implementation architecture.
