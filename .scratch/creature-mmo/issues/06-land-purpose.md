# Decide whether land and construction belong in the core

Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Mode: HITL
Status: open
Blocked by: 04

## Question

What player need would land ownership and construction serve in this game's core loop, and should either be included, deferred, or excluded? If included, decide the minimum ownership/access model and safeguards against finite land preventing latecomers from participating. Do not design a building catalog.
