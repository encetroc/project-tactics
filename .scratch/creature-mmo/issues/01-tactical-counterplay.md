# Define tactical combat and accessible counterplay

Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Mode: HITL
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

What are the baseline battle rules (squad size, turn structure, positioning, action choices, information, and victory conditions), and what makes a strong strategy counterable without relying on higher stats? Use concrete matchup examples; decide how readily a player must be able to assemble or access a counter. Cover the common PvP/PvE foundation, not an exhaustive ability catalog.

## Answer

Resolved through live discussion with the user.

- Players may possess many creatures; combat is capped at 3v3.
- Teams alternate individual creature activations. Each team chooses its creatures' activation order. A creature has 30 seconds to act, then its turn ends automatically.
- Combat uses a Wakfu/Dofus-like grid without elevation. Positioning, movement, range, and line of sight matter.
- Opposing creature information is visible: stats, abilities, passives, and items. Combat should reward informed decisions, not dependence on luck.
- Abilities are procedurally generated from a creature's genome. How the genome generates them is a separate decision in [Define a deep but readable genetic model](02-readable-genetics.md).
- MP/AP/WP was discussed as a possible framework, but is not adopted. The resource framework, ability costs, and any recovery rules are explicitly deferred.
- PvE belongs from the start and shares the combat foundation with PvP. Victory means all enemies reach 0 HP; objective-based victory was not adopted.
- Players theorycraft counters through breeding and item crafting using game knowledge. Planning, time, and resources should reliably yield a viable counter; randomness must not be its sole acquisition path.
- Strong strategies should admit multiple tactical and build responses. A counter grants an advantage rather than guaranteeing victory; no single mandatory counter.

### Follow-up boundary

This resolves the combat foundation, not every playable rule. Which team starts remains deferred. Final resource/action rules and worked concrete matchups remain to be decided after genome-driven abilities are specified; see [Complete combat rules around genome-driven abilities](09-complete-combat-rules.md). No concrete creature matchup or numerical balance was approved in this discussion.
