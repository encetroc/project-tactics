## Question

Given the agreed genome-driven ability model, what detailed resource and action rules govern a creature's activation (including costs, recovery, movement, and restrictions on powerful abilities)? Preserve the separate action-point and movement-point budgets adopted in [Define creatures before their genes](11-creatures-before-genes.md); no special third resource is adopted. Specify the behavior of the agreed core stats, damage/resistance categories, modifier calculations, shared targeting, effect-chain ordering, and passive-trigger loop safeguards. Add stats only where needed rather than assuming an exhaustive catalog. How is the starting team selected, and when do teams choose or revise their creature activation order?

Use concrete contrasting squads to demonstrate multiple tactical and breeding/item-crafting responses to a strong strategy without guaranteed wins or mandatory rare counters. Preserve the foundation in [Define tactical combat and accessible counterplay](01-tactical-counterplay.md). Establish rules and worked examples, not exhaustive balancing or an ability catalog.
