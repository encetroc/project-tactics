# Tactical breeding vocabulary

Glossary for the [current design spec](.scratch/tactical-breeding/spec.md). The previous glossary is retired with the former planning effort; definitions here describe only the new baseline.

**Ability**:
A creature mechanic explicitly played by the player.

**Passive**:
A creature mechanic activated automatically when its conditions are met.

**Creature stat**:
A numerical property of a creature, distinct from an individual ability's parameters.

**Ability parameter**:
A property of an individual ability, such as its base range, area, or damage amount.

**Innate definition**:
A creature's starting properties, distinct from its changing combat state.

**Combat state**:
Changing encounter properties such as position, current values, temporary modifiers, and statuses.

**Conditional effect chain**:
Ordered effect steps whose conditions inspect the state left by preceding effects; a failed condition explicitly skips a step or ends the chain.
